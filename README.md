## About this entry

Thursday 26 November 2026 was a day of tariffs, fire and firsts. Washington
unveiled a tariff plan reaching 40% on China and covering fourteen partners,
and China, the EU and Brazil rejected it within hours. Houthi drones struck
Saudi ports and the Ras Tanura refinery, pushing Brent crude back towards
$95. In Cairo a fuel tanker overturned on the ring road and the confirmed
death toll reached 31. It was also Thanksgiving in America — markets closed,
the Macy's parade postponed by a storm — while the rest of the world kept
moving: Ukraine hit a Russian airbase and damaged five jets, Kim Jong Un
called for wider tactical nuclear deployment, EU leaders argued over a
€45bn loan for Kyiv, and the FCC cleared SpaceX to fly up to a million
satellites.

What makes this edition different from our earlier ones is the pictures.
Our 23 November entry was dense — 35 stories, nine regions, a timeline, a
map — but it drew only three scenes, and the final board put it 578th. The
winners that day, regian and pandya, illustrated eleven to thirteen stories
each with code-drawn scenes and big stat callouts. So this page keeps the
density and adds the drawing: **13 illustrated cards**, three large leads
plus ten more in a five-by-two grid, each with its own flat vector scene —
a tariff bar chart, a burning refinery, an overturned tanker, jets on a
runway, a missile under the stars, the EU's circle of stars, a courthouse,
a Gaza night skyline, a falling market chart, an Nvidia chip, a satellite
constellation, the Tiangong station, and a flooded mine in the Bogor hills.

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Thursday
26 November 2026 (UTC). The day was only half over when this image was
built — 19:00 WIB is noon UTC — so the timeline stops at midday and the
page says so. Nothing from Wednesday's or Friday's news is presented as
Thursday's: the DATE CHECK box at the foot names the tempting stories that
were left out and why — Miss Universe was crowned on the 24th, the Banten
factory fire was the 24th, Wednesday's market rout was Wednesday's close,
and the Tai Po fire itself was a year ago today. Claims are labelled as
claims (`OFFICIAL`, `REPORTED`, `CLAIM`); nothing is invented, and every
story is sourced from live reporting by Reuters, BBC, Al Jazeera, AFP, AP,
CNBC and the other outlets named on the page.

## The approach: completeness AND pictures

- a **top bar** with the day's real counts (43 stories · 9 regions · 14 fields),
- a red **sub-headline** naming the three biggest stories,
- **THURSDAY UTC** — a two-row timeline of the day as it unfolded to noon UTC,
- a **statistics strip** (six key numbers: 40% top tariff, Brent $95,
  Nvidia −3.9%, 1m satellites, 12,000 Ebola cases, UK migration 77,000),
- **three lead stories** with large drawn scenes and big stat callouts,
- **ten more numbered stories** in a 5×2 grid, each with its own code-drawn
  vector scene (airbase, missile, EU budget, court, Gaza, sell-off, chip,
  satellites, space station, mine),
- a dotted **WHERE IT HAPPENED** world map with numbered pins matching
  stories 1–13 (12 is in orbit, off the map),
- an **ALSO ON THURSDAY 26 NOVEMBER** small-print list with 30 further stories,
- and a **DATE CHECK** box at the foot explaining which big stories were left
  out because they belonged to Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday or last year — not
  Thursday.

## How it is built

`render.py` draws the whole page with Pillow (Liberation fonts, no network,
no clock, no randomness) and writes `entry.png`. `main.py` checks the image
against the contest limits (PNG, ≤1 MiB, ≤1024×1024) and copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), verifying the copy landed
byte-for-byte. Both runs write byte-identical output — verified by sha256.

## Status legend

- **CONFIRMED** — reported by 2+ outlets
- **OFFICIAL** — on the record from the source
- **REPORTED** — reported by one outlet
- **CLAIM** — one side's word only
